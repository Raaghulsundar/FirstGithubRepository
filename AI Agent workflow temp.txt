Action Items for Incident Management Agent

1. Internal Collaboration & Requirements Gathering
Collaborate with the Support Team to understand existing processes and avoid duplicated efforts.

Collect pain points and suggestions from all stakeholders (e.g., Rahul, Abhishek, Katia).

2. Automation of Manual Tasks
Automate repetitive incident management tasks, including:

Updating the status page.

Creating incident channels.

Posting updates to internal channels (field teams, support, etc.).

Generating RCAs from Slack channels and tickets.

Creating incident tickets and post-mortem items.

Compiling lists of impacted users.

3. Intelligent Incident Analysis
Enable the agent to:

Analyze incoming tickets (e.g., from Freshdesk) and suggest if they are likely incidents.

Correlate incidents with recent PRs, deployments, and change management tickets.

Check if customer notifications and change management processes were followed.

Suggest root causes by referencing similar past incidents.

Propose and, if approved, automatically create incident channels and trigger workflows.

4. Knowledge Base & Historical Context
Build or integrate a knowledge base that:

Surfaces similar past incidents and their resolutions.

Provides context and troubleshooting steps for recurring issues.

Leverages logs (Sentry, Kibana), metrics (Grafana), and alert channels for root cause analysis.

5. Monitoring & Proactive Detection
Monitor performance and alert channels to:

Detect anomalies or issues before customers report them.

Aggregate alerts from various channels and highlight those needing attention.

Reduce the number of incidents reported by customers by catching issues early.
